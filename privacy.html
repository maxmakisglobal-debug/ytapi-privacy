ytapi Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026.

ytapi uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Who uses it

ytapi is used only by the owner of the channels Hold or Break and Patent & Powder, for those channels.

Data accessed

With the owner's authorization, ytapi reads and updates the owner's own videos, playlists, thumbnails and comments, and reads the owner's channel analytics.

Use and sharing

The data is used only to publish and manage the owner's videos and to produce analytics reports for the owner. It is not sold or shared with third parties.

Storage

OAuth tokens and reports are stored on the owner's private server with restricted access. Data is kept only as long as needed for these purposes.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions.
